## A fixed plan doesn't know how you feel. AAC does.

Training plans from a magazine or a PDF are the same for everyone and never change. But your life does: a short night, a heavy week at work, a cold, a day when you fly. **AAC repeats a simple, transparent cycle: assess, train, listen, adapt.** Every day and every week.

<div class="numeri not-content">
  <div><b>5K → 42K</b><span>four goals, from your first step to the marathon</span></div>
  <div><b>Every day</b><span>your session adapts to resting HR, HRV and sleep</span></div>
  <div><b>Every week</b><span>the plan moves forward, repeats or steps back</span></div>
  <div><b>0 black boxes</b><span>every change comes with a written reason</span></div>
</div>

<CardGrid stagger>
  <Card title="Listens to your body" icon="heart">
    Every morning it compares resting HR, HRV and sleep with your own 28-day average. One bad signal and the session gets lighter on its own.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Zones that are truly yours" icon="setting">
    Heart-rate zones start from your resting and max HR, not just your age. Every session shows its limit and where it comes from.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Keeps you injury-free" icon="warning">
    It watches your load: if you build up too fast it tells you, and if you overdo it it lightens the session. The long run grows by 10% at most.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Always transparent" icon="open-book">
    A history collects every intervention by the coach, with the reason. Notifications reach you even when the app is closed.
  </Card>
</CardGrid>
