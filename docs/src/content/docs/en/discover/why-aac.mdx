**Always Adaptive Coach** was born from a simple frustration: training plans don't know how you feel. They say "Tuesday, 40 minutes" even after four hours of sleep, and make you repeat the same week when you're ready to move up. AAC does the opposite: **every day it looks at your data and decides with you.**

## The cycle that makes the difference

1. **Assess** — on your first visit it reads your history and suggests the week to start from. If you already run, you don't start from scratch.
2. **Train** — it gives you the session of the day: duration, structure, heart rate to respect and a guide pace.
3. **Listen** — it compares resting HR, HRV and sleep with your personal average, checks your load and looks at how your runs went.
4. **Adapt** — it lightens a session, repeats a week, adds a run once you've earned it. And it always writes down why.

## Five things a fixed plan can't do

- **The session changes with you.** One signal — resting HR 5 beats above your average, HRV down 15%, under five and a half hours of sleep — and the session drops to about 60%. Hard sessions become 25 easy minutes.
- **The plan moves on when you're ready.** Every week the coach decides: forward, repeat or one step back, based on how many sessions you did and how your heart responded.
- **Your zones are yours.** The heart-rate limit starts from your resting and max HR (Karvonen method), with a safety cap. Not from your age alone.
- **It protects you from injuries.** Recent vs usual load, weekly km jump, long-run jump: three signals with clear thresholds taken from research.
- **No black boxes.** Every decision has a written reason, in the app and in the coach's history. The rules are declared and explained on these pages.

## Who it's for

- **People starting again**, or who have never run: you begin with run-walk, short bouts and plenty of patience.
- **People who already run** and want a goal: 5K, 10K, half marathon or marathon, with a plan that adjusts itself.
- **People with a watch** who want that data to actually mean something.

## What AAC is not

It's not a doctor and it doesn't replace a flesh-and-blood coach. It's not a social network and it doesn't sell your data. It's a coach with clear rules, built on endurance-training science (Daniels, Seiler, Gabbett and others), making sensible decisions for you every day — and explaining them.
