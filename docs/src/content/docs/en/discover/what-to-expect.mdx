Here's how the first weeks really go.

## Day one

Sign in with Google, connect your Intervals.icu account and choose **goal, intensity and days** with the coach's guide. AAC reads your history and suggests where to start, with a preview of the plan and the estimated race date. Pick the day of your first session and you're ready.

## Your first week

Run with your watch as usual: runs arrive on their own (automatic sync every 15 minutes and whenever you open the app). Completed sessions light up in the calendar. If your watch didn't record a session, you can mark it as done by hand.

If you slept little the night before or your resting HR is high, today's session gets lighter: **that's not a bug, that's the coach doing its job.**

## The coach's first decision

At the end of the week comes the first decision: **moving forward**, **repeat** or **one step back**, with the reason. You'll find it in the Plan's adaptations, in the coach's history and, with notifications on, on your phone.

## From week three

With three weeks of data the coach estimates **the date your goal becomes realistic** from your real rate of progress, not the ideal plan. After three runs with at least 10′ of actual running (intervals too, walking excluded) it computes your **guide pace** from your efficiency: with run-walk it comes as the running parts get longer. And it starts adjusting intensity on its own.

## The phases of the plan

| Goal | Weeks | Phases |
|---|---|---|
| 5K | 16 | Restart (run-walk) → aerobic base → speed → race |
| 10K | 22 | Restart → aerobic base → build → taper and race |
| Half marathon | 26 | Restart → aerobic base → build → peak, taper and race |
| Marathon | 36 | Restart → aerobic base → build → marathon block → taper and race |

The weeks are a guide: if you repeat a few, the plan gets longer, and that's fine. **Better to get there healthy than fast.**

## A coach's advice

- **Run easy when the plan says easy.** If you can speak in full sentences you're in the right zone.
- **Don't make up for missed sessions by doubling up.** The coach reorganises them on its own.
- **Sleep.** Sleep is your best coach, and AAC knows it.
- **If you feel pain, not just fatigue, stop.** AAC is not a doctor.
