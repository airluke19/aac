AAC is organised in six tabs, from the most important to the most technical. Data always comes first: big numbers, few words, explanations one tap away.

## Today

First how you are today (resting HR, HRV, sleep vs your average), then your next session with duration, running minutes, **suggested HR** and guide pace; your last run and your week. If the coach lightened the session you spot it right away by a pulsing dot: one tap shows why, what was planned and what changes today. Under "How it goes" you find the full structure and **why that heart-rate limit**.

## Health

Resting HR, HRV, sleep, steps, weight and BMI, VO₂max, blood oxygen and everything your watch sends. Every value has its chart, the comparison with your average and a full explanation: how it’s measured, reference values, what changes it and how the coach uses it, in sections that open with a tap. The **Days with incomplete recovery** card shows the last 30 days on which the coach eased your session, and why.

## Plan

The **calendar** with every session up to race day: done, missed, planned, adapted. Tap a day to see the session, the actual pace, your HR while running and the details. If a day has several runs (a watch that stopped, a double session) you swipe through them with the arrows. Below are the **adaptations** week by week and the **step-by-step guide** to review goal, intensity and days with the coach.

## Runs

Your history in a clean list, one group per month: date, weather, km, pace and zone at a glance, with filters by month and shoe. If two apps upload the same run, you see it only once. Every run has its own page: **satellite map**, heart rate, pace, cadence, power, elevation, running dynamics, running and walking parts, weather and the shoe you wore (with its kilometres adding up). Press ▶ on the map and **relive your route in 3D**: the camera follows you from start to finish, with live kilometres, time, pace and heart rate, and a final summary.

## Share

From a run's page create an **image** for a post or a story (in the colour you like) or the 3D **route video**, recorded on your phone. One tap and it's on Instagram, WhatsApp or wherever you want.

## Progress

Your goal with the earliest possible date, your **predicted race time**, race-day form, aerobic efficiency, weekly volume and the **Load and injuries** section with your recent vs usual load.

## Info

How the coach follows you, explained once and for all. It starts from your heart: max HR (measured or estimated with Tanaka) and resting HR become, with the Karvonen method, **your** easy limit, worked out in front of you with your own numbers. Then the rules for every morning, every week and every 3 weeks, and the deep dives with sources. No secrets.

## And more

- **Coach’s take**: after every run the coach tells you how it went, like a real coach. Was it the planned session, did you respect your heart-rate limit, how is your efficiency, did you set a record, plus **one concrete tip to improve**: cadence, opening pace, recovery, sleep. Every tip is based on proven principles, such as Seiler’s 80/20 rule and cadence research. It arrives as a notification and stays in the run’s detail. No external service: it’s the coach’s rules applied to your data.
- **Sessions on your watch**: in your profile, under Intervals.icu, a switch sends the next 7 days of the plan to your Intervals calendar, with warm-up, repeats and heart-rate limits. If the coach changes a session, it updates by itself. Intervals forwards them to Garmin Connect; with an Apple Watch you need an app that reads your Intervals calendar. [How](/en/start/watch-workouts/)
- **What the coach did**: the clock icon at the top opens the history of every intervention, with the reason.
- **Notifications**: your morning session, an alert every time the coach changes something, the Sunday recap.
- **Copy for AI**: a session's data in Markdown, ready to paste into your favourite assistant.
- **Calendar export** (.ics) for iPhone, Google or Outlook calendars.
- **Light and dark theme**, **Italian and English**, 26 accent colours.
