<Steps>

1. **Create a free Intervals.icu account**

   [Intervals.icu](https://intervals.icu) is the platform AAC reads your data from. It's free and works with almost every watch.

2. **Connect your watch to Intervals.icu — directly, not through Strava**

   Activities imported from Strava can't be passed on to other apps (Strava's terms forbid it). Follow the guide to [connect your watch](/en/start/connect-watch/).

3. **Sign in to AAC with Google**

   Open [aac.run](https://aac.run) and sign in with your Google account. AAC is invite-only: your request reaches the administrator, who approves it.

4. **Paste your Athlete ID and API key**

   On intervals.icu open **Settings → Developer Settings**: copy the **Athlete ID** and generate an **API key**. Paste them into your AAC profile. The key is stored encrypted.

5. **Choose goal, intensity and days**

   The coach's guide takes you through four steps: goal, intensity, days and plan preview. AAC reads your history and recommends where to start.

6. **Install the app and turn on notifications**

   AAC installs on your phone from the browser, no app store needed. That's how you get your session every morning and the coach's alerts. [Here's how](/en/start/app-and-notifications/).

</Steps>

Done. Go for a run: the coach takes care of the rest.
