<Steps>

1. **Sign in to AAC with Google**

   Open [aac.run](https://aac.run) and sign in with your Google account. AAC is invite-only: your request reaches the administrator, who approves it.

2. **Choose where your data comes from**

   AAC reads your runs and health data (resting HR, HRV, sleep) from **Intervals.icu**, which is free. [Connect your watch](/en/start/connect-watch/) to Intervals, then on intervals.icu open **Settings → Developer Settings**, copy the **Athlete ID**, generate an **API key** and paste them into your profile, section *Where your data comes from*. The key is stored encrypted.

   Using Strava? Keep it: [here's how they work together](/en/start/connect-strava/).

3. **Pick the day of your first session**

   In your profile, *Your plan* section.

4. **Review the plan with the coach**

   In the Plan tab the guide takes you through four steps: goal, intensity, days and plan preview. AAC reads your history and recommends where to start.

5. **Install the app and turn on notifications**

   AAC installs on your phone from the browser, no app store needed. That's how you get your session every morning and the coach's alerts. [Here's how](/en/start/app-and-notifications/).

</Steps>

Done. Go for a run: the coach takes care of the rest.
