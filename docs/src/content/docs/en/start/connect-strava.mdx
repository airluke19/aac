Great: **leave Strava as it is**. Just connect the same watch to **Intervals.icu** too (free). From there every run reaches AAC and, alongside Strava, keeps going wherever it went before.

## Why not from Strava?

Strava forbids Intervals.icu from passing on to other apps the activities it imported from Strava: if your runs reach Intervals **only** through Strava, they don't reach AAC. And to read directly from Strava, since 2026 Strava requires app developers to pay for a subscription and limits how many athletes can connect.

The fix is the simplest one: runs go **from the watch to both**.

<Steps>

1. **Connect your watch to Intervals.icu**

   Garmin, Coros, Polar, Suunto: one link in the Intervals settings. Apple Watch: an app that uploads your workouts. The steps for each watch are in [Connect your watch](/en/start/connect-watch/).

2. **Keep Strava connected to your watch**

   Nothing changes: runs keep reaching Strava too, with kudos and segments.

3. **Paste your Athlete ID and API key into your AAC profile**

   On intervals.icu open **Settings → Developer Settings**. Only once.

</Steps>

## What you gain

Intervals.icu doesn't bring only runs: it also brings **resting HR, HRV and sleep**. That's what the coach uses every morning to decide whether to lighten the session, and to work out your personal heart-rate zones.

## Good to know

- **Duplicates**: Intervals merges the same run coming from the watch and from Strava; AAC uses the one that arrived directly.
- **Old history** that reached Intervals only from Strava can't be recovered that way: if Intervals offers it, import your history directly from the watch.
- If runs still arrive only from Strava, AAC tells you in **Today** with a notice and the guide to fix it.

<Aside type="note" title="Self-hosting">
AAC can also read runs directly from Strava, with one tap ("Connect with Strava"). On an instance you host yourself you can enable it by creating a Strava app, which requires a Strava subscription: see [Configuration](/en/self-hosting/configuration/#strava). It's not active on aac.run today.
</Aside>
