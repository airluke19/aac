AAC is a web app: it opens in the browser and **installs on your phone with no app store**. Once installed it opens full screen, like a real app, and can send you notifications.

<Tabs syncKey="phone">
  <TabItem label="iPhone">
    1. Open **aac.run** in **Safari**.
    2. Tap **Share** (the square with the arrow) and choose **Add to Home Screen**.
    3. Open AAC from the icon and sign in (the installed app has its own cookies: you sign in once more).
    4. On Today tap **Turn on** in the notifications box, or go to **Profile → Notifications**.

    On iPhone notifications only work with the app on the Home Screen (iOS 16.4 or later): that's Apple's rule.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Android">
    1. Open **aac.run** in Chrome (or Samsung Internet, Edge).
    2. Tap **Install the app** in Profile → Notifications, or **Install app** from the browser menu.
    3. Tap **Turn on notifications** and confirm the permission.
  </TabItem>
  <TabItem label="Computer">
    Notifications also work in your computer's browser: go to **Profile → Notifications** and tap **Turn on**.
  </TabItem>
</Tabs>

## What you get

Notifications are **on by default**: you only need to give your phone permission, with one tap.

| Notification | When | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Morning session** | at the time you choose, only on days with a session | *Run today — Easy run · 40′ · HR < 149* |
| **Coach alerts** | every time the coach changes something | *Lighter session today — Easy run · 25′. Why: only 4h 50′ of sleep. Planned: Easy run, 40′. Today: 25′ instead of 40′ (about 62%). Your plan doesn’t change.* |
| **Sunday recap** | at 7 pm | *2 runs and 12.4 km in the last 7 days. Next: Tuesday, Long run 50′* |

They arrive in the language you use in the app. You can turn them off one by one in **Profile → Notifications**, and every alert also lands in the [coach's history](/en/coach/coach-history/).

Notifications only contain your session and kilometres, **never health data**.
