### How much does it cost?
AAC is **open source**. You have two options:

- **Cloud at [aac.run](https://aac.run)**: nothing to install, just sign in and run. It's **free for all of 2026**.
- **Self-hosted**: install it on your own server and it's **free**, forever. [Here's how](/en/self-hosting/install/).

Either way you need an Intervals.icu account, which is free.

### Which watch do I need?
Any watch that uploads runs to Intervals.icu or Strava: Garmin, Apple Watch, Coros, Polar, Suunto, Wahoo and more. See [Connect your watch](/en/start/connect-watch/) and [Using Strava?](/en/start/connect-strava/).

### I use Strava: is that OK?
Yes, Strava stays as it is. Connect **the same watch to Intervals.icu too**: runs reach both. Activities that reach Intervals only through Strava, however, can't be passed to AAC (Strava's rules). [Here's how](/en/start/connect-strava/).

### I don't have HRV or sleep data.
That's fine: the coach adapts the plan to the sessions you do and the heart rate of your runs. With resting HR, HRV and sleep it gets more precise.

### Today's session differs from the plan. Why?
The coach lightened it: the reason is written on the session and in the coach's history. It's usually short sleep, a high resting HR or load rising too fast.

### I missed a session. Should I make it up?
No. The coach moves it to the remaining days or accounts for it at the end of the week. Don't double up.

### The suggested heart rate seems low.
The limit comes from your resting and max HR. If your max HR is only estimated from your age, enter a measured one in your profile. And the talk test always applies: if you can speak in full sentences, you're fine. Read [Heart-rate zones](/en/coach/heart-rate-zones/).

### Can I change goal mid-plan?
Yes, from the Plan tab with "Review the plan with the coach". You continue from the same phase of the new plan and your runs stay.

### How do I get notifications on iPhone?
Add AAC to your Home Screen from Safari, open it from the icon and turn on notifications. See [App and notifications](/en/start/app-and-notifications/).

### Can I run it on my own server?
Yes: [six-step installation](/en/self-hosting/install/).
