---
title: Privacy
description: Where your data lives and what AAC does with it.
---
AAC was built for runners, not to sell data. In short:

- **One database per user**, on a private server. Intervals.icu keys are encrypted (AES-256-GCM).
- **No ads, no trackers**, no data sold or shared.
- **Transparent decisions**: the plan adapts to your data and every change has a written reason, which you find in the app and in the coach's history.
- **The administrator sees when you use the app** (days and minutes, while you have an account), never what you look at. No IP addresses, devices or pages are recorded.
- **Notifications** only contain your session and kilometres, never health data; they go through your phone's service (Apple or Google).
- **Strava**, if you connect it: AAC only reads your activities and never writes; disconnecting revokes access and deletes the activities that came from Strava.
- **Maps**: the satellite map loads Esri imagery and OpenFreeMap street names, which only see which map areas are loaded.
- **Deletion**: when your account is removed, your database, notification subscriptions and usage statistics are deleted too.

If you run AAC on your own server, [all the data stays at home](/en/self-hosting/install/).
