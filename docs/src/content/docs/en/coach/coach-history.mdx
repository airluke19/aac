A coach worth trusting is a coach that explains itself. That's why **every time AAC steps in, you know**.

Tap the **clock icon** at the top of the app: it opens *What the coach did*, a day-by-day timeline of everything that changed. A coloured dot tells you when there's something new.

## What you'll find

- **Weekly decisions** — *"Week 6: moving forward · 3 of 3 sessions, average HR 128 bpm"*
- **Lightened sessions** — *"Session lightened · Wednesday: resting HR 68 bpm (+6 vs your average)"*
- **Intensity and frequency** — when the coach adjusts them, and why
- **Load warnings** — when you build up too fast
- **Updated heart-rate zones** — the new limits and where they come from
- **Notifications sent** — your morning session, the Sunday recap

Every entry is stored **in Italian and English** and shown in the language you use. With notifications on it also reaches your phone, even when the app is closed.
